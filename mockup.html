参 · The story

From peatlands
to clay

As a small boy I always followed the pull of nature — that was where I felt safe. Spending hours wandering through the wilderness of the village where I grew up, I forgot about the rest of the world.

I am self-taught. I work intuitively, whether painting or working with clay. In Berlin, the community in different studios showed me the tools. The rest is a process of discovery — of listening to my own intuition.

Przemyslaw Golebiewski · ceramist
